The Flow — Roster to Reconciled Payroll

1

Source of truth — who the employees are

The users table (synced from Google Workspace) holds identity, role, team, region. The HR onboarding roster (HR_ONBOARDING_COMPLETE.csv) adds the payroll inputs: gross_amount, pay_type, default_deductions, bank details.

Data inusersroster CSV
POST /api/hr/onboard
2

Onboarding — hr_onboarding_service

Validates user + entity + COA, then in one transaction: updates users (is_employee, bank), creates the HrEmployee (entity + salary account derived from team), the FinanceCounterparty (type=employee), and now — from the salary inputs — the HrCompensation and HrDeductionRule(s). No salary given → onboarded but not yet payable.

Createshr_employeesfinance_counterpartieshr_compensationhr_deduction_rules
daily sync keeps hr_employees ↔ users · monthly trigger ↓
3

Monthly payroll run (DRAFT) — hr_payroll_service.create_run

For each active employee in the entity, reads the active compensation × deduction_rules and computes gross → deductions (employee-borne + employer contributions) → net. Produces one payslip per employee.

Createsfinance_payroll_runshr_payroll_items
review → submit
4

Submit run → post to ledger — submit_run

Aggregates all payslips into one balanced journal entry and posts it (status POSTED): debit salary expense + employer contributions; credit the bank (net pay) + the statutory payables (CPF / Super / tax).

Createsfinance_journal_entriesfinance_journal_lines
bank statement import ↓
5

Bank payment + knock-off — categorization engine, Phase 2.5

When the actual net-salary and CPF/tax payments land in the bank feed, the categorization engine matches them to the posted payroll JE (±2% amount, ±7 days) and marks them Reconciled — closing the loop between accrual and cash.

Matchesfinance_transactions → payroll JE
