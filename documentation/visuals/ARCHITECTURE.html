Audit notes — reuse, canonical choices & dead-code candidates

Reuse / don't rebuild (already exists)

  • AP matching — invoice_service.get_open_for_counterparty (auto 3-case, tested) and get_open_for_match (manual list). Don't re-implement selection logic (the BUG-1 trap).
  • Cross-entity IC JEs — invoice_service._get_ic_codes + create_ap_payment_entries do the receivable/payable pair. Reuse for any cross-entity posting.
  • JE writing — always via journal_service.create; never hand-build journal lines elsewhere.
  • Vendor identity — vendor_matching_service + counterparty_service.sync_employees.

Canonical vs superseded

  • Payroll: hr_payroll_service is canonical. payroll_service retained only for create_payroll_payment_entries (the categorization knock-off); its create_run is superseded (the /api/payroll route was deleted).

Dead-code candidates — VERIFY before deleting (do not delete blind)

  • wise_service.get_business_profile (singular) — refs=1 vs get_business_profiles (used)
