### How the GST is calculated

- **Cash basis.** GST is recognised when cash actually moves, not at invoice date. This is our consistent reporting basis.
- **Two control accounts.** Output GST on cash collected is recorded to GST Payable; input GST on cash paid to GST Receivable. Each amount is one-eleventh of the GST-inclusive value.
- **Per transaction.** Every GST amount is posted against its own transaction, so 1A and 1B are the sum of individual line items, each traceable to a journal entry.
- **Applicability.** GST is applied only where the entity is GST-registered, the account is GST-applicable, and — for purchases — the supplier actually charged GST. Purchases from suppliers that charge no GST (for example overseas or non-registered vendors) are excluded.
- **Excluded from 1A / 1B.** ATO/BAS settlement payments and opening-balance adjustments are excluded, as they are neither sales nor purchases.
