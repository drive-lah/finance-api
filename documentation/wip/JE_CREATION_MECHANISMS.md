# Journal-Entry Creation Mechanisms — Current-State Map

> **Purpose.** Every place the system mints a journal entry, mapped on three axes the state
> machine cares about: **(1) is it created DRAFT or POSTED, (2) what triggers the flip to POSTED,
> and (3) is FX conversion applied (each leg → its entity's functional currency)?**
> This is the input to aligning JE creation onto ONE state-machine principle.
>
> **Provenance.** Agent-authored (Pickle) by reading the code on 2026-08-15 — every row cites a
> `file:line` verified in-tree on branch `260814_payout_module`. Not generated by a runnable
> extractor. Status/FX facts are read from the literal `journal_service.create(...)` call args.
>
> **The primitive.** `journal_service.create(...)` **defaults `status=DRAFT`** and **does NOT
> convert** — it trusts the caller (defaults `fx_rate=1`, `currency=base`, `native_amount=amount`,
> per POL-25). So "DRAFT" and "no FX" are what you get by omission; POSTED and FX are opt-in per
> call site. `journal_service.post_entry(...)` is the only DRAFT→POSTED flip. Reports read
> **POSTED only** (trial balance default; DRAFT appears only with `basis=all`) — so a DRAFT JE is
> invisible to the P&L/BS until posted.

## What the map exposes

**1. No single principle governs Create/Post today.** Eight sites POST at creation, eleven default
DRAFT, and the split doesn't follow approval vs non-approval — e.g. the payroll accrual (#3) is
correctly DRAFT-until-approved, but two *other* payroll paths (#4, #5) POST immediately, and the
claim bill (#6) POSTS at approve with no draft stage. The invoice bill deliberately POSTS at
approval (Gaurav ruling). So "approval ⇒ post" is followed in some lanes and skipped in others.

**2. "DRAFT" with no post trigger = stuck-invisible entries.** Sites #8, #10, #11, #18, #19 create
DRAFT but have **no explicit post step** — they rely on a downstream match/reconcile that may never
run. **Amortization (#19) never posts at all** — `months_posted` advances while the JE stays DRAFT
(a real bug, not just a policy gap).

**2a. TWO SEPARATE historical problems — do not conflate them (corrected 2026-08-15):**
  - **The 72 paired intercompany groups (144 rows) are ALL POSTED** (+4 void), **none in DRAFT.**
    Their defect is *currency*, not status: booked unconverted at `fx=1`, so SG-leg and AU-leg
    carry the same raw number in different functional currencies and won't eliminate on
    consolidation (the POL-141 problem). Verified: `intercompany_group_id IS NOT NULL` →
    0 DRAFT rows.
  - **A different, larger set was never recognized as intercompany at all.** ~77 entries titled
    "Sent money to Drive lah Australia" / "Received money from Drive Lah Pte" — genuine SG↔AU
    cash transfers — have **NO `intercompany_group_id`**; the engine booked each as a plain
    single JE via `_create_simple_entry` (#7), not the paired IC path (#10). Split: **33 DRAFT,
    42 POSTED, ~4 void.** So the "stuck in draft" symptom belongs to *misclassified* transfers
    (a categorization-rule gap: no IC rule fired), NOT to the properly-paired IC entries. Fixing
    this needs a rule/classification pass, separate from the currency re-valuation of the 72.

**3. FX is applied at only 8 of 19 lanes.** Missing (books foreign 1:1 at `fx=1`) — the POL-141
gaps: intra transfer (#8), intercompany (#10), allocation (#11), payroll register knock-off (#14),
cross-entity payroll (#15), claim bill + reimbursement (#16/#6), payroll accrual/direct (#3/#4/#5).
Every one bites the moment a leg's currency ≠ its entity's functional currency.

**4. My POL-144 FX transfer (#9) is the lone POSTED cross-currency lane** — inconsistent with its
own same-currency sibling (#8, DRAFT) until we pick one rule.

## The target principle (to decide, then apply everywhere)

Gaurav's statement: **pre-approval ⇒ DRAFT, post-approval ⇒ POSTED — every JE follows the state
machine; create DRAFT for everything, then post on the trigger.** Turning that into the uniform
contract:

- **Every `journal_service.create` is DRAFT at birth.** No lane POSTs inline. (Removes the special
  cases at #1, #2, #4, #5, #6, #9, #14, #16.)
- **POSTED is reached only by a named state-machine transition:** approval sign-off (invoices,
  payroll, claims), or bank-match confirmation (categorization/knock-off lanes), or — for the
  intercompany **receiver leg** booked at an estimate — the arrival-day true-up.
- **Every lane converts each leg to its entity's functional currency at creation** (hoist the
  `to_functional` pattern already in #1/#7/#12/#17 into a shared helper; kill the `fx=1` shortcut),
  with the residual posting to **7100** where two legs' converted amounts differ.

**Open decisions before implementing:**

- **(D1)** Does "money already moved" (bank-driven lanes #7–#16) count as auto-confirmation that
  posts on match, or should even these wait for an explicit review/post? (Determines whether the
  categorization engine gets a post-on-match step or a review queue.)
- **(D2)** Invoice bill currently POSTS at approve by explicit ruling — keep that as the one
  sanctioned inline-POST exception, or route it through the same "approve = the transition that
  posts" path so the rule is uniform (same outcome, one mechanism)?
- **(D3)** Confirm the intercompany model from the prior discussion: sender leg posts on sight;
  receiver leg is DRAFT-estimate → posts + FX true-up when the counter transaction lands.
